Home — big title and tiles

  • A big "شاگردها" title with today's Jalali date, and two tiles: private athletes, and athletes with no plan in orange.
  • Search and the private/public switch, then one grouped list: initial, name, current plan. Tiles and title take room, so about six names fit.
  • The green centre button starts a new plan: pick the athlete, and her last plan is copied.

Movement picker — one tap to add

  • Search with a category switch. Tapping a result adds it right away with the values picked above the list.
  • The default is "مثل قبلی", the previous movement's values; a preset such as "۳۰ ثانیه × ۳" replaces it for timed work.
  • No match: create the movement from the search text. The bar at the bottom counts what's in the day.
