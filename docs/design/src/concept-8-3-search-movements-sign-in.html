Search — every athlete

  • Typing searches private and public athletes together: public athletes are mostly found by name, when they message about a movement. Results come grouped, with the match in pink.
  • Public rows work like private ones: the day letters open their plan, ready for "which movement is this?".
  • Clearing the search brings back the private/public switch.

Movement library

  • Her movements and the 20 built-in ones in one list, alphabetical, with search and category chips. Built-in ones carry a lock: usable in any plan, not editable.
  • Most of her movements are created from the builder: when a search finds nothing, the last suggestion is "+ افزودن «…»". This screen is for tidying up.
  • "+" adds a movement; tapping one opens it.

Add or edit a movement — a sheet

  • Name, category and a short note. Category chips are the ones already in use; "+ دسته جدید" adds her own.
  • Delete works even when a plan uses the movement: plans that use it keep showing it.
  • A built-in movement opens the same sheet read-only, with no save or delete.

Sign in

  • Mobile and password. There is no signup: coach accounts are made with a CLI, so this is the only screen before home.
  • She stays signed in, so the home-screen icon opens straight to her athletes.
  • Signing out is the one item in a small sheet behind "سلام مربی" on home.
