Home — names with their days

  • The athlete says "B" → she taps B on that row and lands on day B. Tapping the name opens the plan.
  • Rows share one white sheet instead of separate cards, so about nine athletes fit. No avatar and no per-athlete color: the letters need the width.
  • Letters keep their columns, so a four-day plan leaves E's place empty. A row with no plan gets the yellow pill and a button to write one.

Day view — one white sheet

  • Opens on the day tapped on home. Day letters as pills; the open day glows pink.
  • The day is one white sheet: pink number, movement, and under it sets × reps in her wording. A superset gets a "سوپرست" tag and a pink edge.
  • Warm-up and cool-down are cream strips outside the numbering. The nine-movement day fits one screen, so one screenshot is the whole day.

New plan — copy first

  • Every new plan asks where to start, with her last plan for this athlete picked. Its day letters and movement counts show what she's copying.
  • She can also copy another athlete's plan, or start empty.
  • Title and Jalali start date are pre-filled with next month.

Builder — the day, editable

  • The same sheet as the day view. Tap a line and it opens in blush: type, +/− for reps and sets, her presets, superset and move.
  • Type at the bottom, tap a suggestion: the new line copies the previous line's sets × reps.
  • "ذخیره" stays in the header as a pink pill.
