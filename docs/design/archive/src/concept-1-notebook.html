Builder — write it like the notebook

  • Starts as a copy of her last plan; she only changes what's different.
  • Rows stay one line each; the open row gets big steppers, per-leg/per-side, and her common presets (15 × 3 is one tap).
  • Type a few letters at the bottom, tap a hit, the next row appears with the last row's sets × reps.
